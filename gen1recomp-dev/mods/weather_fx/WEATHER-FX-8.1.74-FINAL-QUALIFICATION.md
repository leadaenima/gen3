# Weather FX 8.1.74 Final Qualification

Weather FX 8.1.74 is qualified as the next installable baseline after exact-package replay.

Primary correction: 3D precipitation no longer pulses on/off as the player crosses mesoscale cells. WEATHER FRONTS OFF is now a continuous uniform rain/snow/hail ownership mode across the configured 3D WEATHER DISTANCE. WEATHER FRONTS ON retains regional banding without allowing mesoscale thinning alone to hard-disable an authored active precipitation channel.

All 8.1.73 world anchoring, map-entry reprime, snow accumulation coverage, and snow/blizzard fountain protections remain inherited. No fresh live-game framebuffer capture was produced for this release.
